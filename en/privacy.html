Privacy Quebec Law 25

This site does not follow you.
Here is the rest.

No cookies, no analytics, no ads. Here, in plain terms, is what is collected, by whom, and how to reach us.

01 — This site

rabaskaos.com sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising, or tracking tools. Fonts, images, and scripts all come from the site itself: displaying it makes your browser contact no other service.

The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, in the United States). Like any host, GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. MR Services has no access to those logs. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

02 — When you write to us

The “Write to us” form sends nothing by itself: it opens your email app with the message already written. Nothing leaves until you send it.

We then receive your name, email address, and message. Our email is hosted by Google: your messages are stored on its servers, possibly outside Quebec. We use them to answer you and, if you buy a machine, for the sale and the warranty. We do not sell or rent them, and we never add you to a mailing list. Retention follows the MR Services privacy policy (in French).

03 — On Rabaska machines

Rabaska OS sends MR Services no usage data: no account to create, no telemetry, no advertising ID. Browser telemetry is turned off.

Like any computer, the machine contacts servers for its updates: Ubuntu’s, Mozilla’s, and the MR Services channel. The browsers also reach Google services, for example for safe browsing or protected video playback. Those servers see the machine’s IP address, like any website.

The AI assistant opens Google’s Gemini only if you click it. You sign in with your own Google account: Google’s policy then applies.

For troubleshooting, the machine keeps a diagnostic log on its disk (machine name, user name, system log excerpts). It never leaves over the Internet. It is copied only during after-sales service, when MR Services plugs its diagnostic key into the machine.

04 — Your rights

Person in charge of the protection of personal information: the person who runs MR Services. Write to sebastien@mrservices.ca.

You can ask to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent. We answer in writing within 30 days. If our answer does not satisfy you, you can turn to Quebec’s Commission d’accès à l’information.
